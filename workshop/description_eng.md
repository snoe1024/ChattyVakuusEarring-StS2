[b]Client-side mod: doesn't affect gameplay.[/b]

[b]Vakuu watches everything you do, and won't stop whispering about it.[/b]
Install this mod and pick up the Whispering Earring, and Vakuu keeps butting in on your plays. Beyond reacting to all sorts of plays, he points out your small mistakes, laughs at your bad luck, and mourns over the relics you forgot you had.
He'll egg you on to overspend at the shop, chime in with idle remarks on your card rewards, and pressure you into a deal whenever Demon Form shows up.
Over 500 lines of dialogue in total.
A mod setting also lets Vakuu tag along from the very start, even without the Whispering Earring.

[b]Branch support[/b]
Works on both the default and Public Beta branches.

[b]Requires[/b]
BaseLib (installed automatically as a Workshop dependency).

[b]Languages[/b]
English, Japanese, German, Spanish, French, Korean, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese.
The developer is Japanese, so aside from Japanese, everything is machine-translated. Corrections are very welcome.

We're collecting and rating dialogue submissions for each language on a Google Spreadsheet -- if you're interested, please join in.
[url=https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1VJfimzxGFa3HGw4PM1-nGzZ4dzNeyS7zY-siFZz3Q_8/edit?usp=sharing]Chatty Vakuu's Earring Mod - Localization & Translation Suggestions[/url]
Even if writing new lines feels like too much, you can still vote on the existing lines in your language. Please help us spot and weed out unnatural, AI-written Vakuu lines.
