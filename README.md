# Chatty Vakuu's Earring

A mod for *Slay the Spire 2*.

While you're carrying the **Whispering Earring** relic, Vakuu, the first demon, won't shut up about your plays: pointing out your small mistakes, mocking your bad luck, sighing in sympathy over a relic you forgot you had, egging you into overspending at the shop, running her mouth about your card rewards, and pressuring you into a pact the moment Demon Form shows up.

## Translations: contributors wanted

All of Vakuu's lines were originally written in Japanese. The other languages currently shipped with the mod (English, Spanish, French, German, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean) were produced with AI translation to get the mod out to more players quickly — which means the quality varies, and some lines land awkwardly or miss the original nuance.

We've set up a spreadsheet so anyone can help improve this, no translation experience required:

**[Translation spreadsheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1VJfimzxGFa3HGw4PM1-nGzZ4dzNeyS7zY-siFZz3Q_8/edit?usp=sharing)**

What you can do there:

- **Vote** on existing AI-translated lines (or alternatives other contributors suggested) to flag which ones read badly or feel off.
- **Suggest** a better phrasing or translation for any line.
- **Add new line variations** for specific situations you think could use more variety.

You don't need to translate everything, and you don't need to change anything at all. Just downvoting a line that clearly reads as bad AI output, whenever you happen to notice one, already does a lot to raise the overall quality — small, low-effort contributions are genuinely useful here.
